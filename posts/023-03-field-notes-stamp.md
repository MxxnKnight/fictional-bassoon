## The tell

Every document has a tell. Before you trust a leak, read the object itself:

- **Ink weight** — laser-printed pages carry uniform toner. If all three are present, it's probably a reprint, not an original.
- **Stamp depth** — a real rubber stamp bites the paper. Flat stamps are applied digitally, after the fact.
- **Paper fiber** — hold it to light. Government stock has a watermark you can feel with a fingernail.

## The three-second test

:::memo DESK REFERENCE
Tilt the page 30 degrees under a lamp. Real stamps cast a micro-shadow. Scanned stamps don't. This catches ||roughly half of all forgeries|| we receive.
:::

## Filing protocol

When you submit to the board:

1. Photograph the document, don't scan it — scanners flatten the tells
2. Note the paper size; anything non-standard is interesting
3. Keep the envelope. The postmark is often more honest than the letter.

:::pull
Paper copy, do not digitize.
:::
