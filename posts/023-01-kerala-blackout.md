## What went dark

On Tuesday at **04:17 IST**, three submarine cable landings serving the Malabar coast stopped responding within eleven minutes of each other. The official statement blamed a ||fishing trawler anchor drag|| near the Kochi landing station. Our sources say otherwise.

> They ran the drill twice before. This was the third run, and the first one they didn't announce.
> — Senior NOC Engineer, on condition of anonymity

## The pattern

:::memo FIELD MEMO — DO NOT CIRCULATE
Outage windows line up with unannounced maintenance notices filed under ==Section 8(1)(a)== of the maintenance code. The notices exist. The maintenance does not.
:::

This was not the first time. Compare with file [[023-04|the signal map]] — the splice points cluster exactly where the "anchor drag" supposedly happened.

## What we still don't know

- Who ordered the drill, and under what authority
- Why the backup terrestrial links were also throttled to ==redacted throughput==
- Whether the ||nine-hour gap in the NOC logs|| was deletion or design

Every blackout is a rehearsal until it isn't.
:::

## Sources & method

Our reporting rests on three independent sources inside the landing-station operations chain, plus timestamped BGP withdrawal data[^1]. The trawler cited in the official statement was in dry dock that week[^2].

[^1]: BGP data via public route collectors, 02.12.24 04:00–06:00 IST.
[^2]: Cochin Port Trust dry-dock manifest, week of 01.12.24.
